THE VISUALIZATION PIPELINE

Floor Plan to
Final Render.

Every interior project follows a structured pipeline — from concept to photorealistic render.

01
Floor Plan Analysis
We start with your floor plan, architectural drawings, or sketch. Understanding the space dimensions, layout, and flow.
02
3D Model Construction
Building the full 3D model of the space — walls, windows, doors, ceiling heights, structural elements and furniture placement.
03
Materials & Textures
Applying PBR materials — wood finishes, stone, concrete, fabric, glass, metals. Every surface feels physically real.
04
Lighting Setup
Natural daylight through windows, artificial ambient lighting, accent lamps, and mood-specific light scenarios.
05
Final High-Resolution Render
Production-quality final renders — print-ready resolution, multiple angles, and optional walkthrough animation.
